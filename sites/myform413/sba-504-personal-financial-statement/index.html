SBA 504 Personal Financial Statement: Form 413 for a 504 Loan

The personal financial statement for an SBA 504 loan is SBA Form 413. This short page covers only what the form itself says about 504 loans: which box to check, who completes one, how recent the numbers must be, who signs, and who receives it. Everything here follows the current form (version 05-24, OMB No. 3245-0188, expires 08/31/2027).

Which form a 504 loan uses

There is no separate 504 statement. SBA Form 413, titled "Personal Financial Statement," is shared by several SBA programs, and its first page lists them with a checkbox beside each. The instruction is to "check all that apply." For a 504 loan, check the box labeled 7(a) loan / 504 loan / Surety Bonds.

Where the completed form goes: the CDC

This is the one line on the form that is specific to 504. Under "Return completed form to," the form says: for 504 loans, the Certified Development Company (CDC) processing the application for SBA guaranty. The same box sends 7(a) statements to the lender and surety bond statements to the surety company or agent, so make sure yours goes to the CDC.

Who has to complete one

Under the 7(a) / 504 / Surety Bonds heading, the form says to complete it for:

  1. each proprietor;
  2. each general partner;
  3. each managing member of a limited liability company (LLC);
  4. each owner of 20% or more of the equity of the applicant, "including the assets of the owner's spouse and any minor children"; and
  5. any person providing a guaranty on the loan.

Each person on the list completes a statement of their own. If you are not sure whether you fall into one of these groups, ask the CDC handling the application.

How recent the numbers must be

The form asks for the date the information is current as of and prints the limit beside it: for 504 the date must be within 120 days of submission. Pick one date and use balances and values as of that date throughout the form.

Who signs

The certification is completed by each person submitting the information, and by the spouse of any 20% or more owner when spousal assets are included. By signing, you certify under penalty of criminal prosecution that the form and any supporting information submitted with it are true and complete to the best of your knowledge, and you acknowledge that SBA or its participating lenders or Certified Development Companies will rely on it when deciding on the application.

The form's notice to 504 loan applicants says that knowingly making a false statement is a violation of federal law and could result in criminal prosecution, significant civil penalties, and denial of the loan application.

Quick check before you send it to the CDC

  1. The 7(a) loan / 504 loan / Surety Bonds box is checked.
  2. Everyone on the list above has a completed statement.
  3. The "current as of" date is within 120 days of the day you submit.
  4. Total assets equals total liabilities plus net worth. The form prints "Must equal total in assets column" under the liabilities side.
  5. Amounts are in whole dollars. The form's columns are marked "Omit Cents."
  6. The form is signed and dated, including by a spouse where spousal assets are included.
