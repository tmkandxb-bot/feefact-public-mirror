SBA Disaster Loan Form 413: Who Files, the 90-Day Rule and Where to Send It

SBA Form 413, the Personal Financial Statement, has its own section for disaster business loan applications. This page covers only what is different for a disaster application: which box to check, who has to complete a statement, how recent the numbers must be, who signs, and where the form says to send it. Everything here follows the current form (version 05-24, OMB No. 3245-0188, expires 08/31/2027).

Which box to check

Form 413 is one form shared by several programs. The first page lists them with a checkbox beside each, and the form's instruction is to "check all that apply." For a disaster loan, check the box labeled Disaster Business Loan Application. The rest of the form (assets, liabilities, Sections 1 through 8) is the same as for every other program.

Who has to complete one

Under the Disaster Business Loan Application heading, the form says to complete it for:

  1. each applicant;
  2. each general partner;
  3. each managing member of a limited liability company (LLC);
  4. each owner of 20% or more of the equity of the applicant business; and
  5. any person providing an unlimited guaranty on the loan.

Two words differ from the 7(a) / 504 list on the same page: the disaster list starts with "each applicant" where the other starts with "each proprietor," and it names a person providing an unlimited guaranty where the other says "a guaranty." Each person on the list completes a statement of their own.

The 90-day rule

The form asks for the date the information is current as of, and prints the limit beside it: the date "must be within 90 days of submission for Disaster." The other programs on the form get 120 days, so a statement that is recent enough for a 7(a) or 504 application can already be too old for a disaster application. Pick one date and use balances and values as of that date throughout the form.

Who signs

The certification at the end is completed by "each person submitting the information requested on this form and the spouse of any 20% or more owner when spousal assets are included." The form has two signature blocks, each with a signature, date, printed name and Social Security number.

By signing, you certify under penalty of criminal prosecution that the form and any supporting information submitted with it are true and complete to the best of your knowledge, and you authorize the SBA to make inquiries as necessary to verify the statements and determine your creditworthiness.

The notice to disaster loan applicants

Page 5 of the form carries a notice addressed to disaster business loan applicants. It says two things:

  • Whoever wrongfully misapplies the proceeds of an SBA disaster loan is civilly liable for one-and-one-half times the original principal amount of the loan.
  • Any false statement or misrepresentation to SBA may result in criminal, civil or administrative sanctions, including fines and imprisonment, damages and civil penalties, and suspension or debarment from federal procurement and non-procurement transactions.

The form's Privacy Act statement also lists, as a routine use of the information, obtaining credit bureau reports on disaster loan applicants and guarantors.

Where the completed form goes

For a disaster business loan, the form says to return it to the Disaster Processing and Disbursement Center:

  • Mail: 14925 Kingsport Road, Fort Worth, TX 76155-2243
  • Fax: 1-202-481-1505
  • Email: disasterloans@sba.gov

These are the details printed on the 05-24 form.

Quick check before you send it

  1. The Disaster Business Loan Application box is checked.
  2. Everyone on the list above has a completed statement.
  3. The "current as of" date is within 90 days of the day you submit.
  4. Total assets equals total liabilities plus net worth. The form prints "Must equal total in assets column" under the liabilities side.
  5. Amounts are in whole dollars. The form's columns are marked "Omit Cents."
  6. Any attachment is identified as part of the statement and signed, as Sections 2 through 4 require.
  7. The form is signed and dated, including by a spouse where spousal assets are included.
