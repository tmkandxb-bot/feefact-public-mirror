What is not included

MyForm413 is a form-filling tool. It is worth knowing what it does not do before you pay.

  • No advice. We do not give financial, tax, accounting or legal advice, and nobody reviews your figures. The checks are arithmetic: they tell you when numbers do not add up, not whether a value is the right one to report.
  • No decision on your application. A balanced, complete statement does not guarantee a loan approval or any other outcome. That decision belongs to your lender and the SBA.
  • No signature and no submission. You sign and date the form yourself and send it where your program requires.
  • No other forms. The download is SBA Form 413 only. It does not include the rest of a loan application or any supporting documents your lender asks for.
  • No SBA affiliation. MyForm413 is a private tool and is not affiliated with the U.S. Small Business Administration.

When doing it yourself is the better choice: your finances are simple, you are comfortable with the official PDF, and you do not mind adding the columns by hand. The blank form is on the SBA site: SBA Form 413, Personal Financial Statement.

When a CPA is the better choice: you are unsure how to value an asset, how to treat jointly owned property or a business interest, or you want a professional to stand behind the numbers. A form-filling tool does not answer those questions.

When MyForm413 fits: you know your numbers and want them on the official form, added up and cross-checked, without building the totals by hand.

Pricing questions

Do I pay before I start?

No. Filling in the form is free. You pay only when you download the completed PDF.

Is there a subscription?

No. It is one payment per statement.

Do I need an account?

No account or password. At checkout you enter an email address for your payment.

I found a mistake after I paid. Do I pay again?

No. One payment covers one statement. Correct the mistake and download the same statement again at no extra charge.

My business has more than one owner. Is it one payment for all of us?

No. Each person completes their own Form 413, and each completed statement is paid for separately.

Does paying improve my chances of approval?

No. The tool helps you hand in a statement that is complete and adds up. It has no influence on the lender's or the SBA's decision.
