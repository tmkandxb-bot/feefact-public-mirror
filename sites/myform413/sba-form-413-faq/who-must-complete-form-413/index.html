Who Must Complete SBA Form 413?

It depends on which program box you check on page 1. For most loan applications, the short answer is: every proprietor, general partner and LLC managing member, every owner of 20% or more, and anyone guaranteeing the loan. Each of those people fills out their own form.

The lists below are taken from page 1 of the official SBA Form 413 (version 05-24, OMB No. 3245-0188). One business can therefore need several Form 413s, one per person.

7(a) loan, 504 loan or surety bond

The form says to complete it for:

  • each proprietor;
  • general partner;
  • managing member of a limited liability company (LLC);
  • each owner of 20% or more of the equity of the applicant, "including the assets of the owner's spouse and any minor children";
  • any person providing a guaranty on the loan.

Disaster business loan

The list is similar, but not the same:

  • each applicant;
  • each general partner;
  • each managing member of an LLC;
  • each owner of 20% or more of the equity of the applicant business;
  • any person providing an unlimited guaranty on the loan.

Unlike the 7(a) list, this one starts with each applicant rather than each proprietor, the 20% owner line carries no note about the assets of a spouse or minor children, and the guaranty line names only an unlimited guaranty.

WOSB and 8(a) programs

Here the form is not tied to an ownership percentage. For the Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) Federal Contracting Program, it must be completed by each individual claiming economic disadvantage. For the 8(a) Business Development Program, it is submitted by each socially and economically disadvantaged individual using their 8(a) eligibility to qualify the firm.

What about a spouse?

7(a), 504 and surety bonds: the form does not list a spouse as a separate filer. A 20% or more owner's statement includes the spouse's assets, and the certification on page 4 is completed by "the spouse of any 20% or more owner when spousal assets are included." A spouse who owns 20% or more, or who guarantees the loan, is on the list in their own right.

WOSB and 8(a): the spouse must complete a separate Form 413, unless the individual and the spouse are legally separated. For 8(a), a separated applicant provides a copy of the separation document.

More detail: when a spouse must sign Form 413.

If you are not sure you are on the list

The form does not spell out every ownership structure. If yours is not clearly covered, ask whoever is processing your application. The form names them as the place to return it: the lender for 7(a) loans, the Certified Development Company (CDC) for 504 loans, and the surety company or agent for surety bonds.
