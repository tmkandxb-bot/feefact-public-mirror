How Recent Must SBA Form 413 Be?

Short answer: the date on your statement must be within 90 days of submission for Disaster, or within 120 days of submission for 7(a), 504, SBG, 8(a) BD and WOSB. That is what the official SBA Form 413 (version 05-24, OMB No. 3245-0188) prints on the form itself.

The window, by program

Program on the formStatement date must be within
Disaster90 days of submission
7(a) loan120 days of submission
504 loan120 days of submission
SBG (surety bond guarantee)120 days of submission
8(a) BD (Business Development)120 days of submission
WOSB (Women-Owned Small Business)120 days of submission

Where the date goes

On page 2, under the Business Type line and above the Assets and Liabilities columns, the form has a field labeled "This information is current as of (mm/dd/yyyy)". Directly under it the form prints: "(must be within 90 days of submission for Disaster or within 120 days of submission for 7(a)/504/SBG/8(a) BD/WOSB)".

This is the date your figures describe: the balances, values and debts you list are as of that day. It is a separate field from the date beside your signature on page 4.

How to count it

The form counts from submission, so work backward from the day you will hand the statement in, not the day you started filling it out. A statement that is current as of March 1 reaches day 90 on May 30 and day 120 on June 29.

If the date has fallen outside the window, the statement is too old for that program. Bring the figures up to a new date, check that the totals still balance, then sign and date it again.

WOSB: the form also asks for updates

For the WOSB Federal Contracting Program, the form adds that each individual claiming economic disadvantage "must update the form as changes arise, but at least annually, to ensure the information is current, accurate and complete."

The form is the authority here. Check the wording on your own copy: the footer of every page should read "SBA Form 413 (05-24)". Source: the official SBA Form 413 PDF, linked from the SBA's Form 413 document page.
