Security and privacy: where your Form 413 answers go

A personal financial statement holds your assets, your debts and, if you choose to type it, your Social Security number. This page says plainly what happens to that information on MyForm413, what we do receive when you pay, and how to remove what is kept on your device.

🔒
The short version: your form answers stay in your browser. We have no account system and no server copy of what you type into the form. When you pay for a download, we receive your payment email and an order record, and nothing from the form.

Your form answers stay in your browser

The form is a page of JavaScript that runs on your device. As you type, your answers are held in that page's memory. The balance check and the totals are calculated there.

The PDF is built on your device too. Your browser downloads the blank official SBA Form 413 (version 05-24) and a PDF library from this site, writes your answers into the form, and hands you the file. The filled-in form is not sent back to us.

That is also why there is no account: with no server copy of your answers there is nothing for a login to open. It has a cost you should know about. We cannot restore a form you lost, and we cannot look up what you entered if you ask us.

What we receive when you pay

Filling in the form is free. Downloading the completed PDF is paid, and payment needs a few things from you that the form itself does not.

  • Your email. Checkout asks for one. It is stored with your order.
  • A random token. Your browser generates it at checkout and uses it afterward to ask whether your order has been paid. It is a random string. It is not made from your form answers and contains none of them.
  • An order record. The price, the currency, whether the order is pending or paid, the reference numbers the payment provider gives the order, the email the payment provider reports for the paid order, and the payment notification it sends us.

Payment is handled by Waffo. When you continue to checkout, you leave this site for Waffo's checkout page and enter your card details there. Waffo then tells us whether the order was paid. What Waffo does with the information you give it is covered by Waffo's own terms and privacy policy.

Like any website, this one is delivered by a hosting provider (Cloudflare), which handles the ordinary requests your browser makes for pages and files. Those requests do not contain your form answers.

What is kept on your device

  • In the page's memory: your answers while the form is open. Reloading or closing the tab clears them.
  • In the tab's session storage: a copy of your answers in two cases, so that you do not have to type them again. One is when you start checkout, so your answers are still there when you come back from the payment page. The other is when you send figures from the net worth calculator to the form. Session storage belongs to that one tab, and your browser removes it when the tab is closed.
  • In the browser's local storage: the order numbers and tokens for checkouts started in this browser. This is how the browser recognizes a download that has been paid for. It holds no form answers, and it stays until you delete it.
  • The PDF you downloaded: an ordinary file in your downloads folder. It is yours to keep, move or delete.

Delete your local data

You can remove what MyForm413 has stored in this browser at any time. There are three ways:

  • Close the tab. This clears the answers in memory and the tab's session storage.
  • Use the button below. It deletes everything MyForm413 has stored for this tab and in this browser's local storage.
  • Use your browser's own settings to clear site data for myform413.com.

This also removes the record this browser uses to recognize a download you have paid for. If you have a paid download you still need, save the PDF first.

Deleting local data does not delete the order record on our side, and it does not delete a PDF you have already saved.

Check it yourself

You do not have to take this page's word for it. Open your browser's developer tools, choose the Network tab, and fill in the form. You will see the page, its scripts and the blank PDF being loaded. You will not see your answers going out. At checkout you will see requests to this site that carry your email, the random token and the order number, and nothing from the form.

Sensible precautions

  • On a shared or public computer, close the tab when you finish and delete the downloaded PDF.
  • The Social Security number field is optional. You can leave it blank and write the number on the printed form by hand.
  • The downloaded PDF is not password-protected. Store it and send it to your lender the way you would any document with your financial details in it.
