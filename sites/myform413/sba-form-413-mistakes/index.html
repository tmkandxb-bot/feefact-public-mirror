SBA Form 413 Mistakes: A Pre-Submission Checklist

Your personal financial statement is filled in and you are about to send it. Take ten minutes and run through this list first. Every item below is something the official SBA Form 413 (version 05-24, OMB No. 3245-0188) prints on its own pages, so you can check each one against the form in front of you.

The three checks to make first

1. The numbers balance. On page 2, the liabilities column ends with Total Liabilities, then Net Worth, then a Total. Under that Total the form prints: "Must equal total in assets column." If total liabilities plus net worth does not equal total assets, the statement is not finished.

2. The date is inside the window. The "This information is current as of" date must be within 90 days of submission for Disaster, or within 120 days of submission for 7(a), 504, SBG (surety bond guarantee), 8(a) BD and WOSB. Count from the day you will actually submit, not the day you started.

3. It is signed. Page 4 has a signature, date, printed name and Social Security number line for you, and a second set for a spouse. A statement with an empty signature block is not complete.

Page 1: the right person and the right program

  • A program box is checked. The first instruction on the form is "Check all that apply": 7(a) loan / 504 loan / Surety Bonds, Disaster Business Loan Application, WOSB Federal Contracting Program, or 8(a) Business Development Program.
  • Everyone who owes a form has one. For 7(a), 504 and surety bonds the form is completed for each proprietor, each general partner, each managing member of an LLC, each owner of 20% or more of the equity of the applicant, and any person providing a guaranty on the loan.
  • Spouse and minor children's assets are included where the form says so. For a 20% or more owner under 7(a), 504 and surety bonds, the form says the statement includes "the assets of the owner's spouse and any minor children."
  • WOSB and 8(a): the spouse has a separate form. Under both programs the spouse must complete a separate Form 413 unless the individual and the spouse are legally separated. For 8(a), a separated applicant provides a copy of the separation document.
  • It is going to the right place. The form says to return it to the lender for 7(a) loans, the Certified Development Company (CDC) for 504 loans, and the surety company or agent for surety bonds.

Page 2: the header and the summary

  • The header is complete: name, home address, city, state and zip code, phone numbers, business name of the applicant or borrower, and business type (Corporation, S-Corp., LLC, Partnership or Sole Proprietor).
  • The figures are personal, not the business's. The 8(a) instructions put it this way: complete the form "with Personal Information not Business Information" and divide jointly owned assets and liabilities, as appropriate, with a spouse or others.
  • Cents are left off. Both columns are headed "(Omit Cents)."
  • Both installment lines show the monthly payment. Installment Account (Auto) and Installment Account (Other) each have a "Mo. Payments" box beside the balance.
  • Automobiles are described with year, make and model in Section 5, as the Automobiles line asks.
  • Other Income is described. If you entered an amount on that line, the box under the income table is filled in. The form notes that alimony or child support should not be listed there unless you want it counted toward total income.
  • Contingent liabilities are not skipped. The four lines are As Endorser or Co-Maker, Legal Claims & Judgments, Provision for Federal Income Tax, and Other Special Debt.

Pages 3 and 4: every summary line has its detail

Most lines on page 2 carry a "Describe in Section…" note. If a line has an amount, the section it points to should not be empty, and the two should agree.

  • Notes Payable to Banks and Others → Section 2: noteholder names and addresses, original balance, current balance, payment amount, frequency, and how the note is secured or endorsed.
  • Stocks and Bonds → Section 3: number of shares, name of securities, cost, market value, date of quotation, and total value.
  • Real Estate and Mortgages on Real Estate → Section 4: each parcel listed separately, with type, address, date purchased, original cost, present market value, mortgage holder, account number, balance, payment, and status of the mortgage.
  • IRA or Other Retirement Account, Accounts & Notes Receivable, Automobiles, Other Personal Property and Other Assets → Section 5. If anything is pledged as security, Section 5 also asks for the lien holder's name and address, the amount of the lien, the terms of payment, and a description of any delinquency.
  • Unpaid Taxes → Section 6: type, to whom payable, when due, amount, and the property any tax lien attaches to.
  • Other Liabilities → Section 7, described in detail.
  • Life Insurance – Cash Surrender Value Only → Section 8: face amount and cash surrender value of each policy, the insurance company, and the beneficiaries.

Signature, attachments and edition

  • The spouse has signed where required. The certification is completed by each person submitting the form "and the spouse of any 20% or more owner when spousal assets are included."
  • Each signature has its date, printed name and Social Security number.
  • Every attachment is labeled and signed. Sections 2, 3 and 4 say that each attachment must be identified as part of the statement and signed.
  • Nothing is left out. The form's own instruction is to complete it "in its entirety," attaching a separate sheet if necessary.
  • You are using the current edition. The footer of every page should read "SBA Form 413 (05-24) Previous Editions Obsolete," and the top of page 1 shows an expiration date of 08/31/2027.
Read the certification before you sign. By signing, you certify under penalty of criminal prosecution that the information is true and complete to the best of your knowledge. If you correct a number after signing, check the totals, the date and the signature again.
