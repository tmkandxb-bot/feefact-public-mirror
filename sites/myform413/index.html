FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this the official SBA Form 413?

Yes — the PDF you download is the current official SBA Form 413 (version 05-24, OMB 3245-0188), filled with your answers. MyForm413 is a private tool and is not affiliated with the U.S. Small Business Administration.

Who has to complete one of these?

Every owner of 20% or more of the business, for SBA 7(a), 504 and many conventional business loans. Lenders often ask spouses and guarantors too. Each person completes their own statement.

Is my financial data uploaded or stored?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent to or stored on a server. Close the tab and your data is gone. That is also why there is no account to create.

What if my statement doesn't balance?

The tool checks as you type: total assets must equal total liabilities plus net worth, and the Step 5 schedules are cross-checked against your summary totals in Steps 2–3, with warnings on the review step before you download.

How long does it take?

About 15 minutes for most people. It goes faster if you have your bank and retirement balances, property values and loan balances in front of you.

Do I have to enter my Social Security number?

No. The form asks for it, but many people prefer to leave it blank here and write it on the printed form by hand. Either way, it never leaves your browser.
